## 2. Budget Verification

$$\text{Total Cost} = ₹39,950 \le ₹40,000\text{ (Budget Margin: } +₹50\text{)}$$

Every component selected has been validated for:
1. Availability from domestic Indian robotics distributors (Robu.in, Robokits, Amazon.in),
2. Power compatibility with 12.8V LiFePO4 single-bus power distribution,
3. Realistic mechanical suitability for outdoor slope traversal in Mandi.
